# Travel Planner — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13_

Travel Planner is a local-first app. **It collects no data.**

- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- Your profile, trips, Compare picks and queued brochures are stored
  **only on your device** (iOS: the app's private storage; web: your
  browser's local storage). They are never transmitted to us or to any
  third party.
- The app makes no network requests of its own, beyond loading the
  destination photos in the web edition. Tapping a booking link opens the
  seller's own site (Expedia, Google Flights, Epic Pass) with your dates
  pre-filled; that service's own privacy policy applies there.
- A brochure is sent only when you queue one for the assistant and
  provide an email address; the queued brochure (trip summary + that
  email address) is then sent from the assistant mailbox
  (partheysmusebot@gmail.com). Nothing else is ever sent.
- Deleting the app (or clearing the web edition's site data) deletes all
  of its data. There is nothing on a server to delete, export or
  correct — because there is no server copy.

App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected.**

Contact: Parthey Khanderia — parthey.khanderia@gmail.com

_If a future version adds accounts or a backend, this policy and the App
Store privacy answers will be updated before that version ships._
